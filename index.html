FEATURED PROJECT 01

Retail Demand & Inventory Analytics

End-to-end retail analytics solution that analyzes historical sales and inventory data to identify stockout and overstock situations, evaluate sales performance, measure promotion impact, and support inventory optimization decisions.

Business Problem

The retail business faced challenges including frequent stockouts, overstocked products, limited visibility into product demand, and difficulty understanding promotional and seasonal effects on sales.

What I Built

  • Cleaned and preprocessed retail data using Python, Pandas and NumPy.
  • Loaded the cleaned data into PostgreSQL for structured analysis.
  • Used SQL for exploratory analysis and business-focused analysis.
  • Analyzed inventory, sales, promotions, pricing, demand forecasting and seasonality.
  • Created DAX measures and KPIs in Power BI.
  • Developed a four-page interactive Power BI dashboard.
  • Converted analytical findings into business recommendations for inventory and sales decisions.
73K+ Records
5 Stores
20 Products
4 BI Pages

Key Insights

  • Certain products showed high inventory with relatively low sales, indicating potential overstock situations.
  • Some high-demand products had comparatively lower inventory, creating stockout risk.
  • Revenue and sales performance varied across stores, regions and product categories.
  • Promotion effectiveness differed across product categories.
  • Competitor pricing and seasonal trends were relevant factors in demand analysis.

Banking Fraud Analytics

End-to-end banking fraud analytics solution that processes large-scale transaction data through a Bronze, Silver and Gold ETL architecture before delivering fraud monitoring and business insights through Power BI.

Business Problem

Financial institutions process millions of transactions, making it important to monitor transaction activity, identify fraudulent transactions, understand customer behavior and provide actionable fraud insights.

What I Built

  • Extracted raw transaction data through AWS S3.
  • Built a Python ETL workflow covering extraction, validation, transformation and loading.
  • Implemented Bronze, Silver and Gold data layers.
  • Created PostgreSQL fact and dimension tables using a structured analytical model.
  • Created business-ready SQL views for fraud and transaction analysis.
  • Built a five-page interactive Power BI dashboard.
  • Implemented logging and data validation throughout the ETL workflow.
6.3M+ Transactions

Key Insights

  • Fraud represents a small proportion of total transactions but can have significant financial impact.

CUSTOMER ANALYTICS 03

Telecom Customer Churn Analytics

End-to-end telecom customer analytics solution designed to identify churn patterns, analyze customer risk and lifetime value, identify high-value/high-risk customers, and translate analytical findings into retention strategies.

Business Problem

Customer churn directly affects recurring revenue and long-term customer value. The project analyzes customer behavior to identify the segments most associated with churn and prioritize retention opportunities.

What I Built

  • Built a structured Python ETL workflow for extraction, validation, transformation and loading.
  • Organized data into Bronze, Silver and Gold layers using PostgreSQL.
  • Performed churn, contract, risk and customer-value analysis using SQL.
  • Created customer risk and value segments and lifetime-value calculations.
  • Developed Power BI and DAX KPIs for customer analysis.
  • Built a five-page interactive Power BI dashboard.
  • Converted findings into customer retention recommendations.
44.16% High-Risk Churn
39.50% MTM Churn
₹282.4M Churned LTV
31.3K High-Value/High-Risk

Key Insights

  • High-risk customers had a 44.16% churn rate compared with 15.34% for low-risk customers.
  • Month-to-month customers had the highest churn rate at 39.50%, compared with 24.64% for one-year and 14.71% for two-year contracts.
  • Approximately ₹282.4M in lifetime value was associated with customers who had already churned.
  • Approximately 31.3K customers were identified as both high-value and high-risk.
